# Validation and Qualification Gaps v1

This note lists the external evidence still required after the full v2 roadmap is implemented as repository-native contracts.
It exists to keep deterministic artifacts from being mistaken for empirical qualification.

## Physics and Environment

Required external upgrades:

- full GR geodesic integration for selected compact-object scenarios,
- relativistic plasma and accretion-state modeling,
- thermal-dose integration tied to trajectory and material stack,
- direction-dependent ISM and target-region environment models,
- source-backed dust-tail calibration beyond local micron-class anchors.

## Capsule Stack

Required external upgrades:

- stack-level ballistic-limit testing across projectile size, angle, material, and velocity,
- hydrocode correlation for tens-of-km/s extrapolation,
- material-specific thermal and radiation degradation coefficients,
- mass, center-of-gravity, and thermal closure for the selected physical design.

## Archive Media

Required external upgrades:

- media-specific accelerated aging,
- radiation/TID/SEE/displacement damage tests,
- ECC, redundancy, indexing, and decoding recovery tests,
- explicit separation between physical survival and readable archive recovery.

## Mission Operations

Required external upgrades:

- target-state and ephemeris validity over selected horizons,
- correction authority under realistic power and sensing limits,
- launch architecture, solar-Oberth thermal protection, and propulsion closure,
- legal, regulatory, and operational review outside the repository.

## Review and Publication

Required external upgrades:

- independent reproduction reports,
- independent benchmark comparisons against external physics tools,
- public wording review for overinterpretation risk,
- periodic citation-quality review for source drift.

The repository can track these gaps and block overclaims.
It cannot replace the external work itself.
